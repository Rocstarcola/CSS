Questions

Frequently asked

Are the prizes safe for kids?

Yes. We curate age-appropriate toy prizes and clearly note any items meant for older children. Let us know your typical visitor age and we'll tailor the mix.

How does the refill subscription work?

Pick a frequency — monthly or quarterly — and a fresh bag of prizes ships to you automatically. You can pause, skip, or cancel anytime, no contracts.

Can I put my logo on the box?

Absolutely. We can print your name, logo, and brand colors right into the box for a polished, custom look.

What are the boxes made of?

Durable PLA plastic, 3D-printed in-house. They're sturdy, reusable, and easy to wipe clean.

Do you offer fully custom designs?

Yes! Want a box shaped like your mascot or a special theme? Tell us your idea and we'll design it.

Where do you deliver?

We're starting local with pickup and nearby delivery, and can ship for subscription refills. Reach out and we'll sort out the best option for you.
